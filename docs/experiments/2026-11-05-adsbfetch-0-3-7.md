**Question.** The owner's `adsbfetch` (the separate adsb_data_fetcher checkout) is at 0.3.7. I last tested 0.3.3.3 ([adsbfetch](2026-11-04-adsbfetch.md)). The changes since then that matter to us:
- automatic ADSBx sessions through Byparr on Colima;
- `area --window`, its five-minute publication gate and `--wait-up-to`;
- the 3D `--near LAT,LON,ALTITUDE_M --radius-km`, and `--preserve-observations`;
- cache revalidation and `--recheck-before`, and the fixes for 404 races, rolling traces and HTML poisoning.

The owner asked for three things to be validated first:
1. data correctness of 3D queries, exclusions, altitude references, and aircraft disappearing when networks combine;
2. cache and session reuse, naming every unnecessary request;
3. recent-data completeness, and whether `pending`, `partial` and `available` are accurate.

**Build tested.** `adsbfetch 0.3.7+179fe71a`: the editable install in the fetcher's checkout, which has no `.git`. Nothing was built or installed.

**Rules kept.**
- **Run the way the README's wrapper runs it.** Every call goes from the fetcher's checkout: its own `.env` loader, the automatic session, the default cache and the tool's default pacing (`--interval 5`). Calls are serial, one process at a time, because the throttle holds within one process only.
- **No cookie or token is ever read, copied or printed.** `.env`, the session file and the curl dumps are never opened, and no command line carries a cookie.
- **No changes to the tool or its cache.** No cache clear, no `--refresh` or `--no-cache`, no isolated cache folder, and the source left as found.
- **Public development sites only:** Oyster Bay, the eagle cam, Eclipse ESID 375, Essex (xc990819) and Livermore. No private recordings were included.

## Criteria, fixed before the first fetch

**The queries.**
- **Four networks,** `adsbfi,airplaneslive,adsblol,adsbx`.
- **A recording-sized window:**
  - Oyster Bay 2026-09-08 20:45–21:15Z, with true flights a1dfeb at 20:53:00Z and ace391 at 21:08:41Z;
  - the eagle cam 2024-12-28 19:50–20:10Z, with a2baea at 19:58:28Z and a336e4 at 20:03:53Z;
  - ESID 375 2024-04-07 01:39:34Z for 30 min;
  - Essex 2025-04-20 04:10Z for 30 min.
- **A box about 30–40 km across** around each site.
- **The `--near` height is ellipsoidal,** as the README asks: DEM ground (`dopplertrack.terrain`), plus 1.5 m, plus an approximate EGM2008 undulation. That gives OB −30 m, eagle −22 m, ESID 375 250 m, Essex 103 m and Livermore 85 m. These undulations are good to a couple of metres, which matters nothing at a 4–10 km radius.
- **The driver** is [adsbfetch_0_3_7.sh](../../tools/experiments/2026-11-05/adsbfetch_0_3_7.sh).

**1. 3D correctness.**
- **The recomputation.** From each window's unfiltered output (`*_all`), I recompute every aircraft's minimum 3D distance to the `--near` point over its in-window positions. That uses WGS-84 ECEF, with `alt_geom` (GNSS, ellipsoidal) where present and `alt_baro` otherwise, as the README states.
- **The `--near R` output must hold exactly the aircraft with a minimum ≤ R.** Any aircraft whose minimum lies within the barometric slack of R is flagged as a boundary case, not a failure. The slack is the in-window difference between `alt_geom` and `alt_baro` across the box's aircraft.
- **The true flights.** At Oyster Bay and the eagle cam, both true flights are in the `--near 10` set, with their CPA positions.
- **No aircraft disappears when networks combine.** The union of the four single-network `--near 10` sets equals the four-network set, with no aircraft lost.
- **Each kept track holds an in-radius observation** after the two-second merge.
- **`--preserve-observations`** keeps every source's points, so its point count is at least the merged count, and it adds no aircraft.
- **`--props-only`** drops only aircraft whose type the registry says is a jet or helicopter. Unknown types stay.
- **Altitude reference.** In the output, `alt_geom` and `alt_baro` are never swapped. `alt_is_geom` agrees with which of them is non-null.

**2. Cache and session.**
- **The identical rerun** of a settled window (`*_again`) makes no network request for snapshots or traces, as the README says for settled history. Every request it does make is named from its manifest.
- **A smaller view of the same window** (`ob_shifted`) reuses the snapshots, as "one chunk serves every map area" says.
- **The session.** `auth status` showed the `.env` cookie selected and expired 6 h earlier, and a session file present.
  - The first uncached adsbx request must get a fresh session automatically and succeed.
  - The later calls reuse it: no renewals while more than 5 min remain.

**3. Recent data, at Livermore, today.**
- **Before the gate opens.** A window whose last half-hour closed less than 5 min ago exits 3, prints the retry time, writes `pending` to the metadata, and makes no source request.
- **Once eligible.** A window reports `partial` or `available` together with `next_check_at`. `available` must mean every requested snapshot was read and every selected track has positions in the window.
- **The rerun.** The same window run again after its `next_check_at` either keeps its result or improves it. An aircraft counted as missing earlier must not have been there after all.

## 2. Cache and session

- **Settled history costs nothing the second time.** The identical reruns and the smaller view made no network requests, registry included, and took 5–11 s against 240–472 s cold. The `--near` variants run off the unfiltered call's cache in 1–5 s.
- **The cached 404s are reused for 24 h, as documented.** The Oyster Bay rerun's `next_check_at` is 2026-11-05T22:11Z, the expiry of adsb.lol's 404s for 2026-09-08.
- **The session.**
  - The `.env` cookie had expired 6 h earlier.
  - At 22:05:05Z the first adsbx request obtained a fresh session silently; the session file's time is the only trace of it.
  - Every later adsbx call reused that session, with 0 renewals.
  - The free mirrors and cached runs record `session_used: false`.

## 3. Recent data

- **Before the gate.** Both windows exit 3 with `pending` (`awaiting_replay_publication`) and the right retry time, 22:05 and 22:35, and make no request. `--wait-up-to` waits for the gate, then runs.
- **After it.** All three Livermore runs report `partial`, and in each case the only cause is a track with no height inside the radius: 1, 1 and 2 tracks. Snapshot coverage is complete, and every selected aircraft has in-window positions on at least one network.
- **The rerun 22 min later changed nothing.** It found the same 49 aircraft and kept the same 25, with the same status.
- **That rerun still re-downloaded a lot.** It made 203 requests: 118 full re-downloads (42 MB), 76 HTTP 304s and 9 404s.
  - In the first run, 127 of the 191 traces with positions already extended past the window's end.
  - The 9 404s were traces that had answered 200 before. Their positions were kept, as the 0.3.6 fix promises.

## Findings, ranked by practical importance

1. **Aircraft on the ground can't pass `--near`.** A ground observation has no height, since the barometric field reads `ground`, and a point without height can't qualify in 3D.
   - At Livermore 8 kept tracks had ground points inside 10 km, and 1–2 tracks per window were dropped for having only those.
   - At an airfield site the filter therefore drops taxiing and run-up aircraft, which are among the loudest sources a recorder hears.
   - *Smallest fix:* for an `on_ground` point with no height, use the horizontal distance; the aircraft is at ground level, within terrain relief of the microphone. Label it `ground_horizontal` in the metadata.
2. **Trace requests on a day a network doesn't have.** adsb.lol has no replay files for 2026-09-08: both slots returned 404, and the log says so. It was still asked for all 91 aircraft's traces, and all 91 returned 404. At 5 s per request that's 7.6 min of a free service's capacity for nothing.
   - *Smallest fix:* when every requested replay slot of a network for a day is 404, skip that network's trace lookups for that day, or probe one trace first. Say so in the log and metadata.
3. **A recheck of a closed window re-downloads today's rolling traces.** The 22:27 rerun of the 21:30–22:00 window made 118 full downloads (42 MB) and 76 revalidations, and changed nothing.
   - *Smallest fix:* treat a provisional trace as settled for the query once its cached copy has positions after the window's end, plus the 60 s discovery tolerance.
4. **`partial` gives no reason.** At Livermore the only cause was a track with no height in the radius. That's permanent, yet it reads the same as missing snapshots, and `next_check_at` is null. The log line says it; the result doesn't.
   - *Smallest fix:* add `result.partial_reasons`, for example `["unknown_altitude:1"]` or `["missing_snapshots:adsblol"]`. Consider `available` with a warning for the permanent kind.
5. **`auth status` names the expired `.env` cookie as the selected one.** Commands in automatic mode skip it and use or obtain a saved session. A new session leaves no log line either.
   - *Smallest fix:* have `status` report what automatic mode would use, and that the environment cookie has expired. Log one line when a session is obtained.
6. **A few repeated requests**, 7 of 321 at Livermore. A TIS-B address got a 404 and was asked again 10 s later on three networks. Two traces that had answered 200 were asked again 5 s later; the 404s that came back were correctly ignored.
   - *Smallest fix:* within one run, reuse a 404 that is seconds old.

**Missing features, ranked.**
1. **A `--near` prefilter from the replay sightings.** Every discovered aircraft's trace is fetched before the radius check. At Livermore that was 196 lookups (about 7 min) for 25 kept tracks, and at Oyster Bay 364 for 18. Skipping aircraft whose sightings in the window never come within R plus a speed × gap margin would cut most of them.
2. **A per-track barometric correction.** Where `alt_geom` is missing, `alt_baro` stands in, and the two differ by 76–122 m median and up to 470 m at the 95th percentile here. The track's own GNSS-minus-barometric offset nearby would remove most of that near the radius.
3. **A height above sea level for `--near`.** Today the caller must add the geoid undulation (about −32 m in New York, +46 m in Essex); a built-in EGM2008 lookup or an `MSL` option would remove that.
4. **Documentation.** With `--clip-to-box`, observations inside the radius but outside the box can't qualify, because the radius check sees only the kept rows.

## Outcome

0.3.7 returns correct data: the 3D filter, the network merge, `--props-only`, `--preserve-observations` and the altitude fields all check out on four public sites and two known pairs. Cache reuse for settled history is complete, and the automatic session works. What remains is cost and clarity at the edges:
- aircraft on the ground fail the 3D test;
- a network missing a day still gets one trace request per aircraft;
- rechecking a closed recent window re-downloads its traces;
- `partial` and `auth status` don't say what they mean.

Nothing found affects the traces we already use.
