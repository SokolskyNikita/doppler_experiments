# The analysis window, after the overlap finding (proposal)

PLAN Phase 2 item 5, second round. This is a design, written before any code or run.

**decision:** whether the package's window and hop change from the 2.048 s Hann window at a 0.25 s hop.

## What drives it

Item 3's third round ([tracker-likelihood](../2026-11-04-tracker-likelihood.md)) found that per-frame
evidence over overlapping frames isn't independent. Harris's overlap weight w = 1 / (1 + 2 Σ_m ρ(m·hop)²) is the
share of a frame's evidence that is its own; today it is 0.254. The first round of item 5
([window-length](../2026-11-04-window-length.md)) found the opposite pull: a shorter window cuts the
smear on a pass's steep part, while a long one measures the steady tails better.

The arithmetic separates the two:

| Window, hop | w | Bin, ENBW | Frames per minute | Independent frames per minute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.048 s Hann, 0.25 s (today) | 0.254 | 0.49, 0.73 Hz | 240 | 61 |
| 2.048 s Hann, 0.5 s | 0.507 | 0.49, 0.73 Hz | 120 | 61 |
| 2.048 s Hann, 1.0 s | 0.937 | 0.49, 0.73 Hz | 60 | 56 |
| 1.024 s Hann, 0.512 s | 0.947 | 0.98, 1.46 Hz | 117 | 111 |
| 1.024 s Hann, 1.024 s | 1.000 | 0.98, 1.46 Hz | 59 | 59 |
| 0.512 s Hann, 0.512 s | 1.000 | 1.95, 2.93 Hz | 117 | 117 |
| 4.096 s Hann, 2.048 s | 0.947 | 0.24, 0.37 Hz | 29 | 28 |

At a given window, the hop only resamples: about 61 independent frames a minute at 2.048 s, whether the hop is
0.25 s or 1 s. A 1 s hop already gives w ≈ 0.94, not 0.5 (0.5 is the 0.5 s hop). So:
- **What the hop decides:** only whether evidence that is not independent is counted as if it were. A detector or
  tracker can instead weight the dense frames by w, as round 3 did, without losing the 0.25 s sampling that
  `fit_event` uses. The first round found 2.048 s at a 1 s hop worse than the baseline on every parameter.
- **What the window decides:** the real trade. A shorter window gives more independent frames, a coarser bin, less
  per-frame gain for a steady tone (4.9 dB falling to 1.6 dB from 2.048 s to 0.512 s, per the first round), and
  less smear where the sweep rate g is high. The matched window is 1/√g, about 0.9 s at Oyster Bay's event 2.

## Candidates

1. **C0, today:** 2.048 s Hann at 0.25 s.
2. **C1:** 2.048 s Hann at 1 s (w 0.94): the same bins with independent frames.
3. **C2:** 1.024 s Hann at 0.512 s (w 0.95). Hann at half overlap sums to a constant, so every sample counts
   once.
4. **C3:** 1.024 s non-overlapping blocks (w 1), in two forms:
   - **C3h, Hann:** wastes the tapered edges, about half the data's weight.
   - **C3r, rectangular:** uses all the data, but its −13 dB sidelobes leak strong lines.
5. **C4, a multi-resolution pair:**
   - **Long:** 4.096 s Hann at 2.048 s (w 0.95, 0.24 Hz bins) for the flat tails.
   - **Short:** 0.512 s Hann at 0.512 s (w 1, 1.95 Hz) for the steep middle.
   - **Rule, fixed now:** each frame takes the window whose matched sweep rate 1/T² is nearer, in log, to the local
     |df/dt| of `ridge()` on the long image. This is Ferguson & Quinn 1994's and Iatsenko's point that the
     resolution should follow the instantaneous chirp rate.

   `detect` and Tier 1 use the long image; `ridge()` and the fits use the composite.

Every candidate keeps the 0.488 Hz grid (`nfft`), so frequencies compare directly. Each frame's evidence is
weighted by its w wherever a statistic sums frames.

## Measurements, synthetic first

1. **Synthetic ROC**, on 4xlarge boxes sharded by candidate, before any real recording is read:
   - **Inputs:** round 3's noise (2 h of white noise, 150–250 Hz windows) and synthesised passes. The passes are
     audio from the propagation delay alone (PLAN item 2's oracle), at three geometries (straight at 600 m and
     1500 m, and a 1 km pass with g at Oyster Bay event 2's peak), three seeds, and amplitudes 0.06, 0.03 and
     0.015 in 0.4 noise.
   - **Measures, per candidate:**
     - `detect`'s ROC: detection probability against false alarms per hour on the noise;
     - `ridge()`'s median error and bridges against the known curve;
     - `fit_event`'s f0, v and d errors.
   - **Gate, fixed now:** a candidate goes on to real data if, at 1 false alarm per hour, its detection
     probability on the 0.03 passes is no lower than C0's, and its `ridge()` median error is at most 1.05× C0's.
     C0 always goes on as the baseline.
2. **Real data**, for the candidates the gate passes, each with its own spectrograms built from the recipes (as
   the whitening run did):
   - **Livermore:** `ridge()`'s error and bridges on the 29 primary passes, plus item 5's fits (contrast and
     brightness, f0, v and d), with the flagged 11 reported.
   - **Tier 1:** false alarms per hour on the background spans at each candidate's own threshold, with
     `--shards 11` on 4xlarge boxes.
   - **Tier 2:** the saved-fit margins and ranks on the positives.

## Criterion

- **Item 5's, as its first round fixed it.** For each objective and each of f0, v and d, the median |relative
  error| is at most 1.05× C0's, on the 29 primary passes (f0, v and d) and on the development events (v and d). The
  paired sign test is reported.
- **Tier 2:** no loss on the four positives, meaning ranks no worse and margins no smaller.
- **Reported, not judged:** Tier 1's false alarms and `ridge()`'s bridges. They inform item 3's next round, which
  waits on this one.
- **Retune:** one counted retune after the first result, written down before it runs.
- **More than one candidate passing:** the highest w wins, then the longest window.
