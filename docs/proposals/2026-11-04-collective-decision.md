# One next decision: can a frozen collective search earn a verdict?

**Status, 2026-11-04:** proposed acceptance contract; experiments remain paused. This document does not
authorize execution. It supersedes the next-step interpretation of the historical collective proposal,
while retaining every earlier failure. See the [evidence correction](../experiments/2026-11-04-research-reset.md#current-review-2026-11-04).

**Hypothesis.** Scoring trajectory-shaped curves jointly at a shared receiver location and clock can retain
evidence lost by straight-pass event fits and separate the true solution from whole-search competitors.
Existing selected-cell diagnostics motivate this hypothesis; they have not tested the complete claim.

**Decision.** Prepare one corrected, versioned collective procedure and ask the owner to choose a bounded
successor research budget before any scientific execution. The proposed feasibility budget below is
not yet authorized. This budget choice, rather than another partial search, is the next owner decision.
Do not adopt v3, call v4 measured, reopen a failed fitter/tracker round, or acquire a new corpus to answer
this first question. Scientific execution requires a separate authorization after the freeze below.

## Required freeze

Before freezing or executing, inventory a feasible date/source frame and its complete trace coverage.
The historical roughly 120-day frame cannot supply 100 selection plus 300 independent validation draws.
Do not treat resampling, repeated audio, source variants or repeated traffic days as new independent units.
The first decision is a **complete development separation study** on the existing frame; later 1%
certification requires its own feasible disjoint frame and authorization, not 400 new days as a prerequisite
for learning whether this mechanism works at all.

Pin source, lock, case/input hashes and the actual statistic before any new scientific results are read.
The successor starts from dense v4: 60–300 Hz, harmonic 1, top 10 usable 240 s detections; 1 km coarse and
200 m fine grids; best 120 s support; ±25% fine tone search; the current dense coarse sweep; 2 spread-unit
margin capped at 20 cells with the declared top-three fallback; 15 °C, weather off. Record every sweep,
sample count, validity/weak-window rule, normalization seed and clock setting in machine-readable metadata.
The inherited tone/CPA currently come from **refined** detections; acknowledge that dependence rather than
claiming the pre-refinement interface was implemented.

Engineering dependencies are explicit one-pass/one-window assignment, retention of multiple clocks at a
point, all-cell mode merging, complete-unit enforcement, and reader/writer schema agreement. Distinct modes
for this successor are separated by **more than 2 km OR more than 20 s in clock**, with deterministic greedy
suppression in descending score order. The numeric clock rule is a new design choice, not a validated
historical rule. Retain sufficient candidates for every clearing mode, rather than a fixed top-five list.
Own and null must use the identical finite search and mode rule; neither is claimed exhaustive in continuous
position/clock space. Known-answer fixtures must check selector correctness, serial/pool equivalence,
missing units, cap behavior, and alternate clocks/peaks against a bounded exhaustive reference.

Use the historical clock-free rule for this round, including the midnight right-day requirement. Require
equal relative-day trace coverage and envelopes for own/null, including second-day data when applicable.
A metadata gate, weather change, new detection support or changed selector after freeze is another version
requiring new own/null results. Missing coverage or any setting mismatch blocks a scientific judgment.

## Fixed acceptance and stopping

**Label dependency before scoring:** the current midnight case has `true_pair: null`. The historical
four-positive association criterion is retained, but is not presently evaluable for that case. Establish
reference associations independently of any successor winner before execution, or explicitly preregister a
different evaluable successor criterion. Do not fill labels from search results or silently omit this check.
No audio labeling or association experiment is authorized during this reset.

1. **Complete search:** all eight historical development cases, every retained fine cell, all mode outputs
   and completion/hash manifests. Four positives represent two source groups, not four independent trials.
   Each positive's winning mode must be within 2 km **and include both true flights**. The midnight case
   must select the correct UTC day. No chosen true cell, changed-grid refinement or incomplete maximum counts.
2. **Research feasibility budget, proposed for owner approval:** every own-day and wrong-day unit's complete
   warm-cache search must finish within **120 minutes on a pinned 4xlarge**, including coarse/fine scoring
   and merge; record instance,
   workers, CPU and wall time. Stop a case at the cap and mark it incomplete, rather than spending beyond
   the approved bound. This is a separate successor gate. The original **180 s release-budget criterion
   remains NOT MET** and cannot be satisfied retroactively by this revision. Existing v3 Oyster metadata
   records 606.891 s for coarse scoring and 142.745 s for one fine cell; it does not measure v4's runtime.
   The historical dense-v4 estimate of roughly 70 minutes coarse/two hours total is a reported projection,
   not a result. No dense scientific run may start until the owner accepts this cap or approves a different
   bound in the contract before results. Scientific feasibility would still leave release-speed work open.
   Apply the same cap to positives, controls and nulls; timeouts are incomplete, never nonexceedances.
   Before requesting approval, publish the exact unit list/count and total campaign envelope. Eight own
   cases plus at most 50 days for each of eight cases means an upper bound of 408 units / **816 box-hours on 4xlarge** at the cap, before any identical-null reuse or unavailable-day reduction. This is a worst-case
   spending bound, not a runtime estimate or an authorization. The owner must approve the actual smaller
   inventory and its total cap; retries, additional dates and later certification need separate allowance.
3. **Development separation diagnostic:** freeze each case's available historical wrong-day list and its
   count before scores, using at most the historical 50 selected days per group (45 for Essex after its
   originally declared missing releases), preserving declared day/coverage rules. Do not substitute days
   after seeing results. Use the empirical .99 `higher` quantile of complete-search maxima as a diagnostic
   threshold; at these counts it is the maximum, **not a validated 1% threshold**. Gumbel fits are diagnostics
   only. A complete no-candidate draw has score −infinity; a missing/interrupted
   draw is incomplete, never silently dropped or replaced after seeing scores. Exactly one own mode must
   exceed threshold on every positive; none may exceed it on the four controls. Zero/multiple clearing modes
   mean abstention. Score, hash and seal outputs before labels are consulted.
4. **Later conditional certification, a separate decision:** before any 1% claim, inventory and preregister
   a feasible frame with disjoint threshold-selection and at least 300 independent validation units from
   the specified competitor population. Define the validation Bernoulli event as **complete-search maximum
   > frozen threshold**, including draws with multiple clearing modes. Retain complete no-candidate draws
   in the denominator; interrupted draws remain incomplete. Require its exact one-sided 95% upper bound
   to be ≤1% for each claimed case distribution. With zero exceedances, 300 gives 0.994%; dependence invalidates
   that calculation. Shared traffic days,
   fixed audio and source variants do not establish national or independent-site error rates. If independence
   or sample support is inadequate, report the tail unresolved and withhold a calibrated verdict claim.
   This does not prevent the bounded development study from deciding mechanism feasibility.

Stop before execution if the successor budget is unapproved. Stop the scientific judgment on an
incomplete/mismatched procedure or any positive/negative/uniqueness failure. Never tune on validation
maxima. Any failure closes this fixed design: there is **no automatic retune**. A successor requires
explicit preregistration and authorization. An unresolved certification tail blocks calibrated claims,
not reporting of a completed diagnostic separation study. Do not retrofit v1–v4 successes.

## Outcome decisions and concurrent work

- **Development gates 1–3 pass:** the mechanism earns consideration for a research-only integration and
  separately authorized conditional certification/calibration plan. It earns no `located` verdict or 1%
  claim. Independent site/device/day evaluation and region coverage remain separate. The original release
  runtime gate remains unmet; do not claim release readiness or open reserved groups automatically.
- **Recall fails:** inspect the already-produced per-window contributions and pruning diagnostics to decide
  whether evidence is absent or the finite search misses it. This diagnosis chooses a successor; it is no pass.
- **Null/uniqueness fails:** retain abstention and redesign aggregation/assignment. More positive fitting is
  not evidence of false-match control. **Budget alone fails:** decide whether to optimize the same statistic
  or explicitly replace the runtime gate before another run.
- **Parallel preparation now:** finish engineering equivalence/manifest checks, preserve evidence, and freeze
  split/eligibility rules. Keep detector S2 and coherence successors closed pending a demonstrated bottleneck.
  No scientific rehearsals or new data processing are authorized by this preparation.
- **After freeze and separate authorization:** own-day cases and diagnostic nulls can compute concurrently.
  In a later separately approved certification study, sealed validation searches may also compute concurrently.
  Thresholds are fixed from selection data before validation is opened;
  adoption waits for complete parity and every gate. Dependency is on the procedure, not positive scores.

The opportunity models provide no shortcut: a tracked aircraft in modeled range is not a labeled audible
pass, correct flight ID, sufficient multi-pass geometry or a correct location. None of those conversion
probabilities has been measured by the national window percentages.
