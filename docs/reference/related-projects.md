# Related projects

Public projects that attack the same problem, what they report and what [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md) takes from them. The initial review was 2026-11-03, followed by the second wave and the **2026-11-04 branch/PR audit** below. The older in-depth tables retain their historical results; additions and a summary of recent changes appear below.

Evidence was checked separately from claims. In particular, Espaye's two real accuracy numbers and franken's field results are text-supported claims; their actual real-case inputs and result bundles were not found in the inspected public material. Numerous available dataset inputs or a synthetic promo video do not independently verify a claimed real accuracy figure.

**Read their numbers as hypotheses, not findings.** Most locator projects here appeared after colsto's November video. The inspected repository results are self-reported, frequently tuned on Oyster Bay or Livermore, and are not independent replications by us. Some now publish field tests, including failures; this is stronger evidence than a synthetic demonstration but does not establish reliable localization of arbitrary audio. Anything we adopt is recorded in [docs/experiments/](../experiments/README.md) with our own measurement.

**The bar is unseen, natural audio across sites and engine types.** Count correct locations, confident wrong answers and abstentions together, with the actual clock/area priors, missed recordings and uncertainty coverage. Naming an aircraft at a supplied microphone position, stitching several recordings into one, refining a known flight, or reproducing Oyster Bay does not meet that bar. No inspected project demonstrates it yet. Piston, turboprop, helicopter and jet results need separate evidence; adding an aircraft-category label or synthetic jet does not establish support for that engine type.

Our own history is the comparison: three blind tracker rounds failed to replace the baseline ([likelihood tracker](../experiments/2026-11-04-tracker-likelihood.md)); straight-pass fits distort real trajectories ([event fit](../experiments/2026-11-04-event-fit.md)); wrong-day search maxima defeat the proposed verdict ([verdict null](../experiments/2026-11-04-verdict-null.md)); and the [home pilot](../experiments/2026-11-04-seattle-home-pilot.md) exposed weak indoor pickup, an invalid hard-silence assumption and coarse-grid sensitivity. A familiar tracker, covariance formula, weather correction or polished app is not a new solution to those findings.

Licences decide what can be borrowed. MIT allows code with attribution. Repositories without a licence file, DOPPLERGEIST under PolyForm Noncommercial, and franken_doppler_finder under a custom MIT-derived licence with an OpenAI/Anthropic restriction are not treated as MIT code sources. Their public claims are reviewed here; no implementation is copied. GPL tools (readsb, planefence, noisecapt) are run as tools; none of their code enters this repo.

## What we take, and where it lands

| Idea | What they observed | Our adaptation | Where in PLAN.md | Literature | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whitening biases frequency | dopplerprint: a 60 s low-quantile floor partly subtracts a slow ridge that sits in one bin for long, 0.4–0.9 bin off, which its fit turned into +2 to +40 % in distance and up to 4 s in CPA time. It now finds tracks on the whitened image and re-measures each frame on the original complex STFT. A 45 s median floor removed the flat asymptotes of slow passes outright | Our `local_whiten` subtracts each bin's median over a window of about two minutes, so a tone that sits in one bin for more than half the window is removed, asymptotes included. Whitening floor and window join Phase 2's model-error perturbations, which the brightness fit can run today, and item 3 finds tracks whitened but measures them on the original STFT | Phase 2, items 2 and 3 | Quinn 1995 (amplitude weights assume unbiased frequency estimates); Kay I eq. 3.41 | Floor-bias causation is not established here; the [frequency-whitening experiment](../experiments/2026-11-04-whiten-frequency.md) failed its criterion and left the default unchanged |
| A tracker that stops and restarts | dopplerprint: a global Viterbi path was forced across the whole recording and chained consecutive aircraft. Its tracker is local (Smith–Waterman): the accumulated score is clamped at zero, so a path starts and ends where the tone does, and paths are cut at a weak run over 3 s, a rise of over 4 bins in 3 s, or a hop steeper than the ridge's own slope. echolocate's second-order dynamic programme rewards continuity and penalises slope changes | Two lines in `tracks.ridge()`: clamp at zero and subtract a per-frame bias. That is the stopping rule 6.3 says Iatsenko lacks. It needs an unclipped, zero-mean image, which today's clipped `local_whiten` output isn't. The segment cuts are untested heuristics and get their own tests | Phase 2, item 3 | Iatsenko 2016 §6 names the missing termination rule | [Three tracker rounds failed](../experiments/2026-11-04-tracker-likelihood.md); no replacement adopted |
| Scores in units of wrong guesses | unheard: almost every try in its coarse (tone, clock) sweep is a wrong guess, so their median and MAD are the recording's own noise for that aircraft, and the score is the best try in those units. Noise alone then peaks at 3–4, because the best of thousands of guesses wins. echolocate: a stationary-tone null was too easy for smooth clutter to beat, so it compares against a fitted linear chirp on the same support | `search` already sweeps ±8 s and ±5 % f0 for every candidate, so the median and spread of those wrong guesses come for free, and a robust z-score against them puts every pair's score on one scale (S2). The chirp alternative is one input to `detect`'s threshold curve, not a gate: short genuine passes look linear | Phase 3 step B; Phase 2 item 6 | Kay II: a maximum over many cells is not a calibrated statistic; confidence is computed after selection | [Comparable-score test](../experiments/2026-11-04-comparable-score.md): not met, separation inside the null's noise |
| Remove the source aircraft, keep the traffic | unheard: with the audible aircraft removed from the flight data, another aircraft can fit it. Its "strong" threshold sits 10 % above the worst score with nothing to find, set on exactly those runs | A negative control per confirmed flyover: the right day and box with that flight removed and the surrounding traffic kept. Expected: abstain, or locate from the remaining flyovers; a confident answer from a substituted flight fails. Masking the flight's column in the CPA table avoids a rebuild per variant | 5.1 negatives; Phase 3's failure cases | L2 and the ADS-B-coverage risk in section 7 | Not yet run |
| The Livermore corpus | dopplerprint and unheard tuned on it; echolocate used five clips as a frozen test. dopplerprint reports that a straight-pass fit to the noise-free ADS-B curve misses slant range there by a median 14 %, and by 40–90 % on some passes: model error, not estimator error | On disk since 2026-11-03 (`raw/audio/livermore/`, see the data root's `MANIFEST.md`). Phase 2's main real data for fits and tracks: 40 passes with trajectories and published frequency tracks, in constant and varying speed and height. One group, development or calibration only, never evaluation: one site, one recorder, one month, single passes, and three projects have already tuned on it | 5.6; Phase 2 item 2; decision 2 | Ferguson 2016 §6 on manoeuvring aircraft | Audited 2026-11-04 ([livermore-audit](../experiments/2026-11-04-livermore-audit.md)): the recorder point is recovered to under 1 m (within 2.3 m of dopplerprint's), every WAV is the ridge ±5.00 s, and the ridge clock matches ADS-B to about 1 s per pass, so timing below a second needs a per-pass shift |
| Altitude provenance | echolocate keeps only geometric heights and refuses to treat pressure altitude as a height; dopplerprint records an altitude source per point (geometric, barometric with the aircraft's own offset, barometric with neighbours' offset, barometric only) | Our loader now keeps altitude and position provenance and honours the flags. Bit 1 marks a new reliable fix after a reception gap over 20 s, not a stale point; bit 8 marks geometric altitude in slot 3. Geometric ADS-B altitude is WGS-84 ellipsoidal, the Terrarium tiles are orthometric, pressure altitude is neither. A Phase 0 fix keeps the source and honours the flags; the sensitivity test is Phase 2's altitude perturbation | D4; Phase 0; Phase 2 item 2 | None among the papers. The references are readsb's `README-json.md` and the adsb.lol historical-data docs | Provenance/flag fix implemented in Phase 0; see PLAN D4 and the model-error sensitivity results |

Two findings of theirs corroborate ours and need no action:
- echolocate found that the steepest received drop happens at the emission-clock CPA, not CPA plus travel time, and that its old proposal was about 18 s off. That matches [timing-conventions](../experiments/2026-11-03-timing-conventions.md) and section 6.1.
- dopplerprint refines each frame's frequency with Candan's three-sample estimator on the Hann DTFT, iterated three times. It is a candidate for Phase 2 item 3's sub-bin estimate alongside quadratic interpolation and Quinn's estimators.
